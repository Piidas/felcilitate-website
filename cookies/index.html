Cookies

This website sets no cookies

We set no cookies of any kind on www.felcilitate-consulting.com: no technically necessary cookies, no analytics cookies, no marketing cookies. We also store nothing in your browser’s local storage.

The reasons are simple. The website is static and needs no session. We use no analytics or tracking service. Fonts, images and scripts are delivered from our own domain, so no third party can set cookies through this site. The language toggle is an ordinary link between the English and German versions of a page.

Because nothing is stored on your device, no consent banner is required and none is shown. Should this change, we will update this page and the privacy policy before any cookie is set.
