Services

We offer amplified transparency on the status of your projects.

The services we provide establish the basis for better decision making for each stakeholder involved in a CAPEX project, from FEL 1 to commissioning.

All services →

Project Execution Planning & Review

Sustainable project execution plans for the early phases, including modularization, and independent Cold Eye Reviews of projects in execution.

Cost Engineering

Review and benchmarking of capital cost estimates, including the Aspen Capital Cost Estimator (ACCE), against AACE and GAO criteria.

Organisational Development

Workshops in all project phases, from kick-off and team building to Project Controls frameworks and cost estimation processes.

Project Risk Analysis

Identification, evaluation and mitigation of project execution risks; contingency quantified through Monte Carlo analysis per AACE.

Interim Management & Shadowing

Project, construction and commissioning management on an interim basis. Preferred model: shadowing, mentoring your project manager and core team through ramp-up, the first 100 days and critical decisions.
