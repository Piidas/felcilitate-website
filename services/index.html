Services

Our range of services helps you to derisk your projects early on and to manage complexity in execution

We support owners, contractors and investors in large industrial CAPEX projects, from FEL 1 to commissioning. Every service is tied to a recognised standard and, where available, to a tool from our suite. ✓ marks services supported by a FELcilitate standard tool.

01 · Planning and review

Project Execution Planning & Review

Standards and tools: Cold Eye Review · PPMOF · Advanced Work Packaging

We support project teams in developing and executing sustainable project execution plans. The execution of CAPEX projects has become more complex, and with it the implementation risk for every stakeholder. The best moment to reduce that risk is early: in FEL 1 to 3, when the scope definition and the Execution Approach are decided. Projects already in execution we review with an independent Cold Eye Review.

  • Cold Eye Reviews of projects in execution: document review, site visit with structured interviews, findings report
  • Development and review of execution plans, including partnering concepts and consortia
  • Modularization concepts and constructability reviews
  • Consulting services for product standardization and modularization
  • Workshop facilitation
02 · Risk

Project Risk Analysis

Standards and tools: ✓ Project Maturity Assessment (PDRI logic) · ✓ Schedule Health Check · ✓ Risk & Contingency · AACE 44R-08, 57R-09, 65R-11, 113R-20

We identify, evaluate and mitigate project execution risks. Risk becomes manageable once it has been named, quantified and assigned. We assess the maturity of the project, test the schedule and quantify the risks against the base estimate and base schedule, so that contingency becomes a range with a probability rather than a percentage.

  • ✓ Project maturity assessment based on PDRI logic, FELcilitate method with 70 questions
  • ✓ Risk assessments including Monte Carlo analysis; contingency as a P50 / P80 / P90 range
  • ✓ Schedule analysis and critical path management (Schedule Health Check, DCMA 14-Point)
  • Supply chain and execution risk evaluation
03 · Cost

Cost Engineering

Standards and tools: ✓ Cost Estimate Review · GAO-20-195G · AACE 18R-97 · ACCE

We review, benchmark and prepare capital cost estimates in line with AACE. A cost estimate is only as good as its basis: classification, structure, Basis of Estimate and the credibility of the number at the investment decision. Our team includes a Certified Estimating Professional (CEP®) and a certified user of the Aspen Capital Cost Estimator (ACCE).

  • ✓ Due diligence checks of cost estimates through AACE application
  • ✓ Benchmarking and estimate validation
  • Evaluation of project alternatives, e.g. modular vs. stick-built
  • Cost estimation with the Aspen Capital Cost Estimator (ACCE)
  • Independent CAPEX budget review for FID
04 · Organisation

Organizational Enabling

Standards and tools: Workshops · Project Controls · Lessons Learned

We give project teams expert support to achieve critical milestones and objectives. Capability stays in an organisation when it is built with the people who run the projects, so we plan and facilitate workshops at the decisive points of a project and use your own projects as the case material.

  • Workshops for kick-off meetings, gate reviews and cost-to-complete
  • Contract analysis and risk management workshops
  • Value engineering and lessons learned implementation
  • Process improvement
  • Trainings
05 · People

Project Leadership

Standards and tools: Mentoring · Shadowing · Interim Management · PMP®

Leading through complexity and uncertainty, with and alongside your project leaders. We prefer to make your project managers stronger rather than to replace them: we coach them and shadow them through the phases that decide the outcome. Where a role genuinely has to be filled, we take it on ourselves.

  • Mentoring and coaching of project managers
  • Shadowing of project leaders in critical phases
  • Interim project, construction and commissioning management
  • Interface and integration management (ISBL–OSBL)
  • Change management and decision support
Cross-capability
