Project maturity assessment prior to FID

A PDRI-style maturity assessment, FELcilitate method, from two independent perspectives, consolidated in a deep-dive workshop: around 75 % defined, and a prioritised list of what to close before the investment decision.

Success story · Anonymised · FELcilitate Consulting

Context

The client’s project was in the development stage prior to the Final Investment Decision (FID). The client requested an assessment of the project’s maturity to identify unresolved issues and the risk of not fulfilling the project’s objectives.

Methodology

FELcilitate proposed a project maturity assessment in the style of the PDRI. FELcilitate developed its own method on the idea of the Project Definition Rating Index of the Construction Industry Institute (CII): it measures how completely the scope of a major project is defined before FID. 70 elements are rated and condensed into an overall score between 70 and 1,000; as a reference for FID readiness, a score below 200 is used.

The assessment combined independent self-assessment with facilitated discussion. Unbiased individual ratings make differences in perception across the team visible; the subsequent facilitated discussion resolves these differences in a targeted, fact-based way.

1

Two assessment perspectives

The assessment was performed from two independent perspectives: the FELcilitate view, based on the data-room analysis, and the client’s project team.

2

Individual assessment

FELcilitate provided its template with all 70 elements, including description, checklist and rating guidance per element.

3

Independent self-assessment

Both parties rated each element independently (1 to 5 or n.a., with comments) and returned their ratings.

4

Consolidation and score calculation

FELcilitate consolidated both returns, calculated the scores and identified the elements requiring discussion in preparation for the deep-dive workshop.

5

Deep-dive workshop

The flagged elements were discussed jointly along the three sections and then finally rated by FELcilitate.

6

Recommendations and report

From the consolidated assessment and the documented comments, the critical scope areas were identified and prioritised measures derived.

Outcome

The assessment delivered an unbiased status of the project definition in three dimensions: Basis of Project Decision (the business case), Basis of Design (the technical maturity) and Execution Approach (the execution strategy and plan).

Low-scoring elements were ranked critically and each received a recommendation based on experience, industry standards and best practice. The recommendations were segregated into “FID” and “Execution” to give the client’s team a priority of actions. Typical focus areas: the definition of project objectives, the finalisation of an appropriate execution schedule, a binding cost assessment in line with ACCE estimating principles, and an overall quantitative risk assessment.

Completing the measures, in particular the critical recommendations before FID, froze the baselines prior to execution. It gave the project owner and manager insight into the undefined elements and a factual basis to initiate and plan the critical first 100 days of the project.

The result Baselines frozen before execution, and a factual basis for planning the first 100 days.
